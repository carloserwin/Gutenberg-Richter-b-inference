# Tehuantepec Earthquake Catalogue (2017)

The file `BDf2_tehuantepec_2017_100km.rds` contains a processed subset of seismic events recorded during 2017 within 100 km of the epicentre of the September 7, 2017, magnitude 8.2 Tehuantepec earthquake (14.761° N, −94.103° W).

The data were obtained from the **Mexican National Seismological Service (SSN), Instituto de Geofísica, UNAM**, and are used to reproduce the real-data analysis presented in the accompanying paper.

**Source:** [SSN Earthquake Catalogue](http://www2.ssn.unam.mx:8080/catalogo/)

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.21766/SSNMX/EC/MX

The original catalogue was downloaded on April 27, 2026. Data are reproduced for noncommercial research purposes with acknowledgment of the SSN, in accordance with its legal notice.
